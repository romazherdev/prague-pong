# 10: Reschedules

**What to build:** When a league match is moved, the captain or an admin changes its date and time. Earlier yes and maybe answers become "please reconfirm," and the line-up shows it needs attention.

**Blocked by:** 08

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The team's captain or an admin can change a match's date and time
- [ ] On a date or time change, every yes and maybe for that match becomes "please reconfirm"; no answers stay as they are (covered by a test)
- [ ] Members see "please reconfirm" highlighted in "my upcoming events" and answer again in one tap
- [ ] The match's line-up shows "needs attention" until the captain reviews or saves it again
- [ ] Clash flags (09) are recalculated for the new date
